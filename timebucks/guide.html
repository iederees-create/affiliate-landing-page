THE BEGINNER'S FIELD GUIDE

Start smart.
Stay realistic.

A simple, independent guide to setting up your account, choosing online reward tasks and avoiding common mistakes.

Visit TimeBucks ↗
01 / THE BASICS

Know what you're signing up for.

TimeBucks is a rewards platform where eligible members may earn small amounts from surveys, short offers and other available online activities. It isn't employment. Availability changes with your country, profile, task supply and device.

Important: Never trust a website that promises guaranteed daily earnings from microtasks. No guaranteed return applies here.
02 / ACCOUNT SETUP

Create one genuine account.

  • Use our clearly marked referral link to open the official TimeBucks website.
  • Register with accurate information and follow any identity or age requirements.
  • Verify your email or identity if TimeBucks asks you to.
  • Read the terms and set up your payment preferences inside TimeBucks.

Don't create multiple accounts, misrepresent your country, or use VPNs to manipulate availability or eligibility.

03 / SURVEYS

Be prepared to screen out.

Survey providers often look for a very specific audience. You might answer a few screening questions and discover you aren't eligible. This is frustrating but normal. Respond honestly and don't repeatedly change demographics just to qualify.

04 / OFFERS & APPS

Read the full offer terms.

Before accepting app install, game, signup or trial tasks, check what the advertiser requires and whether purchases, subscriptions, tracking or app permissions are involved. Skip anything you don't understand or feel comfortable with.

Never share passwords, one-time codes, bank logins or sensitive documents with unofficial contacts claiming to guarantee rewards.
05 / PAYMENTS

Check your actual balance.

Rewards may be pending while an advertiser reviews activity. TimeBucks determines what is eligible, when it is credited, applicable withdrawal thresholds and what payout methods are available in your country. Check its dashboard for current rules rather than relying on figures posted elsewhere.

06 / REFERRALS

What happens when you refer a friend?

TimeBucks currently advertises a 10% lifetime referral commission on qualifying referred-user earnings, subject to program rules. Commissions may be delayed, denied or adjusted for nonqualifying activity. Promotional signup bonuses are not guaranteed.

Do not self-refer, create fake accounts or pressure people to join. Be open about affiliate relationships when sharing links.

07 / A GOOD ROUTINE

Build habits, not expectations.

  • Choose a short time window rather than endlessly checking for tasks.
  • Compare the time required against the potential reward.
  • Keep a record of completed offers and follow up through official support if rewards fail to credit.
  • Walk away from unclear requests or offers requiring unusual upfront payments.
