GOOD QUESTIONS

The things you
should know.

Clear answers, without the hype.

Is TimeBucks really free to join? +

The platform is generally free to join and offers tasks with potential rewards. Read individual offer terms carefully; some third-party promotions may involve optional spending or subscriptions.

Will I actually earn money? +

Some members receive rewards for qualifying tasks. What you can earn depends on location, availability, eligibility and approvals. No amount of income is guaranteed, and this is not a replacement for employment.

Do I have to refer other people? +

No. You can use TimeBucks for available task opportunities without referring anyone. Referrals are a separate, optional program.

How does the referral program work? +

TimeBucks currently advertises 10% commission on qualifying referral earnings, subject to its policies. Referral bonuses, when offered, are conditional. Actual commission and referral status are tracked in TimeBucks, not on TaskTrail.

Why might I get screened out of a survey? +

Research providers look for people meeting specific demographic criteria. Screening out can occur even if you answer accurately. Never provide false information to try to qualify.

Is this the official TimeBucks website? +

No. TaskTrail is an independent information and referral website. Our signup buttons take you to TimeBucks using an affiliate link, which may earn us a commission.
